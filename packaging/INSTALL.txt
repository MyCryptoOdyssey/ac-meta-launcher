META LAUNCHER for Asheron's Call (End of Retail ACE servers)
=============================================================
An in-game window that lists VirindiTank metas in tabs (Quest, KTs, Crafting, Misc,
My Metas) and loads the one you pick. Includes 139 metas from FlyingRagnar's collection.

YOU NEED: Decal, VirindiTank (comes with VirindiViewService), UtilityBelt (0.2.7 or 1.0).

INSTALL (close all AC clients first)
1. Copy everything in the "VirindiTank" folder of this zip into your VirindiTank folder
   (usually C:\Games\VirindiPlugins\VirindiTank). Nothing in it overwrites your own metas
   unless you already have files with the same names.
2. Copy the "MetaLauncher" folder into your Decal Plugins folder, so you end up with
   C:\Games\Decal Plugins\MetaLauncher\MetaLauncher.dll
3. Double-click MetaLauncher.reg in that folder and click Yes (registers it with Decal).
   If you put the folder somewhere other than C:\Games\Decal Plugins\MetaLauncher,
   open the .reg in Notepad and fix the "Path" line first.
4. Open Decal, find "Meta Launcher" in the plugin list and make sure it is TICKED.
5. Start AC and log in. The Meta Launcher window appears (Virindi icon).

USING IT
- Click a tab, click a meta, read the yellow requirement lines, click Load.
- "Whole fellowship" ticked = loads on all your clients (/ub bc). Most metas are
  fellowship metas: make the fellowship first. The fellowship leader leads
  (no Egg needed).
- Many quest metas need a portal tie (Primary Portal Tie) to a Town Network portal.
- Stop VTank / Start VTank turn VirindiTank off/on. Meta buttons such as !continue send
  the meta's own commands.
- My Metas lists every other .met in your VirindiTank folder.
- "After a quest, switch to": select a meta, click "Use selected". Quest metas load it
  when they finish.
- Loading also turns on UtilityBelt's VTank.PatchExpressionEngine (the metas need it).
- "(draft)" metas were never published by their author and may be incomplete; they are
  hidden unless "Show drafts" is ticked.
- Problems: see MetaLauncher\errors.txt and the chat window.
